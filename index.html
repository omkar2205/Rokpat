About Rok

Creating is what drives me, day after day.

Rok Pat is a Lithuanian director, digital creator and filmmaker based in Birmingham. His practice grew from years in art and architecture into a multidisciplinary approach spanning moving image, CGI, design and real-time production.

The work often sits between atmosphere and narrative: cultural references, architecture, human psychology and emerging technology folded into visual experiences made to be felt, not just viewed.
